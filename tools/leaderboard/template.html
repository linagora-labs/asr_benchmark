Open French-Centric ASR Leaderboard

Benchmark of open-weight speech recognition models on French, and on some European languages and Arabic.
Unlike many benchmarks limited to read speech, it also covers spontaneous and conversational speech (meetings, web videos, earnings calls).

A suggestion, a question, or a competitive model to add? Please open an issue on GitHub.
